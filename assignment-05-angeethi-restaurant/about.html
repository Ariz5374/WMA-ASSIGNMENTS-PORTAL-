About Angeethi

Cooked over real fire, plated without shortcuts.

Angeethi opened as a single charcoal grill on a Karachi street corner. Today it's a full smokehouse, but the rule hasn't changed: if it isn't touched by real flame, it doesn't go on the menu. Every burger, skewer, and pizza still passes over the same open coals we started with.

Our Story

Where it started, and where it's going

Our Story

Started at one angeethi

The restaurant is named after the charcoal brazier our founder cooked on before Angeethi had four walls — that same fire is still the heart of every kitchen we run.

Our Mission

Flavor without shortcuts

We cook everything to order over real charcoal or a wood-fired stone, never a shortcut appliance, so every plate tastes like it came off an actual fire.

Our Vision

A smokehouse in every city

We want Angeethi's style of open-fire cooking on a table near you — same coals, same recipes, same standard, wherever we open next.
